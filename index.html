Campsites

Sombrio Beach (East) — Night 1

What's there

  • Bear-resistant food cache
  • Pit toilet
  • Tent pads
  • Creek water source (filter first)

Long classic beach with lots of tent spots. A short walk east along the beach leads to a hidden waterfall — worth checking out before settling in.

Bear Beach — Night 2

What's there

  • Bear-resistant food cache
  • Pit toilet
  • Tent pads
  • Creek water source (filter first)

Three camping areas along the beach — centre sites (near Clinch Creek) are the nicest with some shade; east sites near Rosemond Creek fill up early but sit close to a small waterfall. Good sunset spot.
